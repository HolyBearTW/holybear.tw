<section class="about-section about-process">
  <div class="about-process__statement">
    <p class="about-kicker">HOW I WORK</p>
    <h2>I’m less interested in looking impressive.<br><span>I care whether the result holds up.</span></h2>
    <p>I like keeping the playful, slightly dramatic side of a project alive. But when it comes to features, data, and explanations, the work needs to be honest and specific. Those two sides are not opposites—they are what make the work feel like mine.</p>
  </div>
  <ol class="about-process__list">
    <li><span>01</span><div><strong>Find the real problem first</strong><p>Before adding features, I work out where people are actually stuck and whether the problem is worth solving.</p></div></li>
    <li><span>02</span><div><strong>Build something I would use every day</strong><p>I keep refining the interaction, speed, and visual details instead of treating “it runs” as the finish line.</p></div></li>
    <li><span>03</span><div><strong>Record the outcome honestly</strong><p>I separate what worked, what failed, and what remains unverified so the work still makes sense when I revisit it later.</p></div></li>
  </ol>
</section>
